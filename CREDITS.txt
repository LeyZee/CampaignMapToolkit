CAMPAIGN MAP TOOLKIT
Credits

------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL INFO
------------------------------------------------------------
The aim of this project is to allow Total War modders to create or modify campaign map pathfinding files. The supported files include:
- map.hex
- map_data.esf
- pathfinding.ppd
- trade_routes.ptd
- borders.pbd
- lookup tga images
- and possibly more

The games that the application currently supports are:
- Total War: Rome 2
- Total War: Attila
- Total War: Thrones of Britannia
- Total War: Warhammer
- Total War: Warhammer 2
- Total War: Warhammer 3
- Total War: Three Kingdoms
- Total War: Troy
- Total War: Pharaoh
